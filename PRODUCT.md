## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and engineering hiring managers assessing Ethan Lawrie's software engineering work. The homepage should make his focus clear quickly; case studies explain his own contribution without disclosing employer material.

## Product Purpose

An engineering portfolio supporting interview conversations through concrete work, clear case studies, a public résumé, and LinkedIn contact. Keep positioning role-agnostic; historical job titles remain accurate.

## Positioning

Cloud systems, developer tooling, and operational software lead. Shipped games show product ownership, interaction design, collaboration, and delivery.

## Operating Context

Readers scan the homepage, open a relevant case study or résumé, then contact Ethan through LinkedIn. Support phone and tablet portrait/landscape, keyboard navigation, text enlargement and reduced motion.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, shared CSS, progressive-enhancement JavaScript, three case studies, résumé PDF, and LinkedIn contact. No contact form or public private email/phone.
- Preserve the five routes and current hosting integration. Prepare a preview for review before public publication.
- Keep employer names, accurate roles, broad technologies, personal contributions, and the milestones below.
- Omit internal architecture/workflow sequences, handler details, deployment filters, source, screenshots, records, business rules, scale figures, business-process metrics, internal announcements, and rollout plans.
- Apply this disclosure boundary to homepage copy, case pages, metadata, structured data, and the downloadable résumé.
- Word Lawrie may mention distribution through Coolmath Games, but omit paid/non-exclusive terms. Omit ratings unless verified and dated.

## Brand Commitments

Ethan Lawrie is the public name. Direct, specific writing; no invented outcomes, problem statements, or claims of sole ownership. Word Lawrie’s existing cream, teal, salmon and pale blue palette is the shared website identity, with teal text and rules throughout. IBM Plex typography continues through the Swiss/brutalist refinement.

## Product Principles

Make engineering work easy to understand. Distinguish an individual's contribution from the platform's status. Preserve confidentiality across every public artifact. Let genuine projects and a clear visual hierarchy carry the portfolio.
