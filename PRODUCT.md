# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Ethan Lawrie for software-engineering opportunities. They need to understand his engineering credibility quickly enough to decide whether to invite him to interview.

## Product Purpose

A role-agnostic engineering portfolio that demonstrates Ethan's ability through concrete work, clear case studies, and a privacy-safe résumé. Success means increasing the likelihood of interview invitations without framing the site around particular career stages or employment categories.

## Positioning

Cloud systems and developer tooling lead the story, with observability and SRE-relevant work as a supporting thread. Shipped games provide additional evidence of product ownership, collaboration, interaction design, and delivery.

## Operating Context

Visitors often scan the homepage first, then open a relevant case study or the public résumé before deciding whether to contact Ethan. Public case studies must remain understandable without disclosing confidential employer material.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static web portfolio with a homepage, three public case studies, résumé download, LinkedIn link, and contact form.
- Do not describe the portfolio or Ethan's availability using graduate or internship role labels.
- Keep claims factual and tied to the résumé or public evidence.
- Label planned work, including the CMV 2,000+ employee scope, as planned rather than shipped.
- Keep private phone and email details out of the downloadable public résumé.
- Omit confidential source code, internal identifiers, screenshots, and deployment data.

## Brand Commitments

Ethan Lawrie is the public name. The voice is direct, technically literate, and grounded in evidence; avoid generic hype, inflated claims, and templated AI-sounding copy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current résumé source provided by Ethan; privacy-safe public copy at `assets/resume/ethan-lawrie-resume.pdf`.
- Microsoft Azure CLI Copilot observability case study at `work/azure-cli-telemetry.html`.
- CMV incident-reporting architecture case study at `work/cmv-incident-reporting.html`.
- Word Lawrie release case study and real product imagery at `work/word-lawrie.html` and `assets/wordlawrie/`.
- Portrait at `assets/images/ethan.jpg`.
- No public-safe Microsoft or CMV screenshots, internal telemetry, or deployment artifacts are available; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Lead with cloud systems, developer tooling, and operational reliability.
- Make engineering credibility easy to verify.
- Keep the portfolio role-agnostic and useful across software-engineering hiring contexts.
- Use shipped games as supporting proof of range, not the primary positioning.
- Preserve confidentiality and distinguish planned scope from delivered outcomes.
