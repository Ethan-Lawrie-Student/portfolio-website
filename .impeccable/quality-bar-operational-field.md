# Operational Field — Quality Bar

This is the visual and interaction ceiling for the selected portfolio world.

- Composition: a compact 7/5 cover with an open five-stage operational field; selected work must enter the first 1505 × 1045 viewport.
- Hierarchy: cloud systems and developer tooling lead; enterprise architecture follows; shipped games provide supporting product evidence.
- Material language: warm cream ground, deep teal fields, salmon actions, pale-blue system labels, square geometry, one-pixel rules, and no ornamental depth effects.
- Evidence: only public-safe, résumé-supported claims. No invented dashboard screens, values, metrics, or confidential identifiers.
- Typography: condensed display type for major statements, sans for reading, mono only for system labels and factual metadata; no decorative H1 kickers.
- Motion: restrained, once-only typesetting and system-path reveals using opacity and small transforms; all content remains available with reduced motion or without Motion.
- Responsive states: open vertical crosshair on desktop, compact horizontal path on tablet, legible vertical path on mobile.
- Anti-patterns: no bento grids, floating pills, glows, gradients, pulses, parallax, card lifts, fake terminal chrome, oversized empty hero, or repeated template scaffolds.
