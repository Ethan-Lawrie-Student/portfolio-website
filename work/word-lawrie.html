Case study 01 · Shipped product

Word Lawrie

A mobile word game built by a four-person team, then adapted for a browser audience through a non-exclusive Coolmath Games license.

Role
Lead Developer & Producer
Period
Jan 2023 — Feb 2024
Team
Four people
Stack
Unity, C#
Play on Coolmath Games ↗ View App Store listing ↗

Problem

Traditional word ladders ask players to transform one word into another through valid intermediate words. The challenge was to turn that paper puzzle into a clear, replayable mobile experience with enough variation to support repeated play.

Approach

I led development in Unity and C#, coordinated a team of four, and worked across gameplay implementation, production, UX refinement, and release preparation. Difficulty levels change word length, while modes such as Detour, Timed, and Mastermind vary the core transformation rule.

Key decisions

  • Keep the current and target words visually distinct so the puzzle state remains understandable on a small screen.
  • Build multiple modes around one recognisable mechanic rather than add unrelated minigames.
  • Use a non-exclusive license to reach a browser audience while retaining the existing mobile release.

Result

The game shipped on mobile and was licensed to Coolmath Games. Browser distribution produced a 7,000% increase in monthly active users, turning a small team project into a product used by a substantially larger audience.
