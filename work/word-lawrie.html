The interaction

Players change one letter at a time to move between words. The challenge was making that rule immediately legible while keeping input, feedback, and progression quick enough for repeated play.

What I led

I led a four-person team across core gameplay, user experience, testing, and release. The game shipped on mobile and web with Normal, Modes, and Daily play options.
