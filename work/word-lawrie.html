Design challenge

Word ladders are compact on paper but harder to make legible, responsive, and replayable on a phone. The product needed a clear interaction model and enough variation to support repeated play without diluting the core rule.

What I owned

I led development in a four-person student team and worked across core gameplay, UX iteration, testing, and release. Difficulty settings change word length, while Detour, Timed, and Mastermind modes vary the transformation rule without replacing it.

Product choices

  • Keep the current and target words visually distinct so the puzzle state remains understandable on a small screen.
  • Build multiple modes around one recognisable mechanic rather than add unrelated minigames.
  • Use a non-exclusive license to reach a browser audience while retaining the existing mobile release.

Public release

Word Lawrie shipped across mobile and web. I negotiated a paid, non-exclusive distribution licence with Coolmath Games, where the published game held a 4.2/5 rating from 839 votes when recorded for the résumé.
